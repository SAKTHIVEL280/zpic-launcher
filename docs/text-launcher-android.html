Zpic Launcher

Text-only Android launcher with an A to Z app list

Zpic has a text-only app page: app names from A to Z, with an animated alphabet rail for fast one-handed scrolling.

Questions

How does the A to Z list work?
Swipe up for your apps, then drag along the alphabet rail. Letters swell under your thumb and the list jumps to that letter.
Can I search instead?
Yes. Search finds apps, contacts, app shortcuts and phone settings, and it works as a calculator.
Can the home screen be text-only too?
Yes. Pinned apps can appear as a plain text list that grows as you resize it.
