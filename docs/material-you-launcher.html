Zpic Launcher

Material You launcher for Android with themed icons

Zpic is a Material You launcher with Material 3 Expressive design. It follows your system colours, your wallpaper or a colour you choose.

Questions

Does it follow my wallpaper colours?
Yes. Zpic can use system Material You colours, read colours from the wallpaper, or use your own colour.
Does it work with ColorBlendr?
Zpic reads the system accent palettes, which is what ColorBlendr changes, so your colours carry over.
Are there themed icons for every app?
Zpic uses an app's monochrome icon when there is one. Otherwise it derives one from the icon image, and you can change any single icon.
