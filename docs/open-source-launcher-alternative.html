Zpic Launcher

Open-source launcher alternative for Android

A free, open-source alternative for people comparing launchers such as Nova, Niagara, Lawnchair and Olauncher. No ads, no account, no internet permission.

Questions

Is Zpic affiliated with those launchers?
No. Zpic is an independent project. Those names are trademarks of their owners and are mentioned only for comparison.
What does it offer?
A fine home grid, widget stacks, folders, a dock, four app pages, themed icons, focus modes, and backups you can share.
Is it private?
Zpic has no internet permission, so nothing can leave your phone through it.

More about Zpic

  • Minimal Android launcher, free and open source
  • Text-only Android launcher with an A to Z app list
  • Material You launcher for Android with themed icons
  • Launcher that themes every app icon on Android
