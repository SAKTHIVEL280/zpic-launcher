Zpic Launcher

Minimal Android launcher, free and open source

Looking for a minimal launcher for Android? Zpic is a free, open-source home screen with a clean grid, a text-only app list and no ads.

Questions

What makes a launcher minimal?
It shows less by default. Zpic starts with a clock and a short list of pinned apps. Everything else stays one swipe away.
Can I keep icons?
Yes. You can use names only, icons with labels, custom pages or smart groups, and change at any time.
Does it help with phone use?
Zpic can pause before distracting apps, count daily opens, and hide apps with scheduled focus modes.
