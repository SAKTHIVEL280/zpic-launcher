Zpic Launcher

Launcher that themes every app icon on Android

Zpic themes apps that have no monochrome icon, including icons made from flat PNG or JPG images, in tonal, two-tone, glyph or monogram styles.

Questions

Why do some apps ignore themed icons?
Android themes only apps that ship a monochrome layer. Zpic builds one from the icon when an app has none.
Which styles are there?
Full colour, tonal, surface, two-tone, glyph only in white or theme colour, and monogram, in 7 shapes.
Does it support icon packs?
Yes. Nova-compatible icon packs appear in the Icons settings.
