Zpic is a free, open-source home screen for Android that stays out of your way.

* A fine home grid with pages, widgets, widget stacks, folders and a dock
* Four app pages: names A to Z, icons, custom pages and smart groups
* Themed icons for every app, including ones built from flat images
* Material You colour, 12 clock options and 60 recolourable wallpapers
* Focus modes on a schedule, a pause before distracting apps, and daily open counts
* Search for apps, contacts, shortcuts and phone settings, with a calculator
* No ads, no account and no internet permission
