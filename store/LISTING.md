# Zpic Launcher: store listing

**Short description (80 characters max)**
A calm, Material You home screen with a free grid, smart groups and themed icons.

**Full description**
Zpic is a home screen that stays out of your way. It feels like stock Android, with Material You colour and expressive motion, and everything else is yours to arrange.

- Home grid: place the clock, widgets, apps and folders anywhere, across as many pages as you like
- Four app pages: names A to Z, icons, custom pages, or smart groups
- Themed icons for every app, even ones built from flat images
- 12 clock styles, and 60 wallpapers you can recolour
- Notification shelf, gestures, a dock, and a daily intention
- A gentle pause before distracting apps
- No ads, no account, no tracking

**Category:** Personalization
