# Zpic Launcher privacy policy

Zpic has no internet permission. It collects nothing, and nothing leaves your device.

Everything Zpic stores, such as your layout, settings and recent apps, stays on your phone. If you use Backup, you choose where that file goes.

Optional permissions, each off until you turn it on:

- **Accessibility:** only to lock the screen with your gesture. Zpic reads nothing on screen.
- **Notification access:** only to count waiting notifications per app, for dots and the shelf. Zpic doesn't read their content.
- **Usage access:** only to list recently used apps.
- **Contacts:** only to find people when you turn on contact search.
- **Calendar:** only to show your next event on the home screen.

Notification access also lets Zpic show the music player. Contacts and calendar data are read on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere.
